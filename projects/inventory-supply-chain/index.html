OPERATIONS ANALYTICS

Dynamic Inventory & Supply Chain

Exploring stock movement, availability and supply chain performance through SQL and Power BI.

SQLPower BIInventory Analytics

The business context

Operational teams need visibility into stock levels, replenishment cycles and fulfilment patterns to understand where availability and working capital may be affected.

Questions this project explores

  • Which products or locations show unusual stock movement?
  • Where do replenishment cycles and lead times put availability at risk?
  • How can dashboards support inventory monitoring without implying forecast certainty?

Analytical approach

Inventory structure

Organise products, locations, receipts, demand and movements with consistent units.

SQL metrics

Calculate inventory turnover, stock coverage and other appropriately defined operating measures.

Power BI reporting

Build views of availability, movement patterns and replenishment indicators.

Operational recommendations

Discuss possible actions, uncertainty and the limits of the available data.
