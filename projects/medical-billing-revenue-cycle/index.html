CLINICAL ANALYTICS

Medical Billing & Revenue Cycle

Investigating claims flow, payment status and operational delays using Excel and SQL.

ExcelSQLRevenue Cycle

The business context

Healthcare billing involves multiple handoffs, from charge capture and claim submission to payment and follow-up. Tracking these stages can reveal where administrative work accumulates.

Questions this project explores

  • Where do claims slow down or require rework?
  • How do paid, pending and rejected claims vary across stages?
  • Which operational metrics provide a useful picture of collection workflow?

Analytical approach

Data model

Define clear billing events, statuses and dates using synthetic or appropriately licensed example data.

Excel exploration

Profile claim records and review data quality, ageing and workflow categories.

SQL analysis

Query payment turnaround, denial categories and other well-defined process indicators.

Recommendations

Translate observed patterns into operational questions and improvement opportunities, with documented limitations.
