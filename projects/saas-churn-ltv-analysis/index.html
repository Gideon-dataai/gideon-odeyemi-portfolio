OPERATIONS ANALYTICS

B2B SaaS Churn & LTV Analysis

Investigating retention, customer churn and lifetime value in a subscription business.

SQLPythonSaaS Analytics

The business context

Subscription businesses depend on retaining customers and understanding how revenue evolves. Clear cohort definitions are essential when comparing churn and lifetime value.

Questions this project explores

  • How do retention and churn differ across cohorts and customer segments?
  • Which observed characteristics are associated with churn?
  • How sensitive are customer lifetime value estimates to modelling assumptions?

Analytical approach

Customer and billing model

Organise accounts, subscriptions, transactions and activity into a consistent analysis-ready structure.

SQL cohorts

Build customer cohort and retention queries with documented definitions.

Python analysis

Explore churn signals and lifetime value scenarios while separating descriptive results from prediction.

Business interpretation

Communicate opportunities, limitations and actionable follow-up questions.
