CLINICAL ANALYTICS

HEDIS Quality & Risk Stratification

Exploring healthcare quality measurement and stratification with an emphasis on clear definitions and responsible reporting.

PythonPower BIHealthcare Quality

The business context

Quality measurement requires precisely defined populations, events, denominators and exclusions. A useful dashboard must be understandable and transparent about what each metric measures.

Questions this project explores

  • How do population and measure definitions influence reported performance?
  • Where do performance patterns differ across defined groups?
  • How should missingness and simulated risk indicators be presented without overstating clinical meaning?

Analytical approach

Measure design

Document eligibility, numerator, denominator and exclusion logic for selected example measures.

Preparation and validation

Prepare a clearly sourced or synthetic dataset and check measurement consistency.

Stratified analysis

Use Python to explore quality indicators and group-level patterns, carefully distinguishing associations from risk predictions.

Visual communication

Create Power BI views that put definitions, caveats and transparent comparisons alongside key metrics.
