HOW IT WAS BUILT

From raw data to decisions

Python generated 4,060 synthetic rows, including 60 intentional duplicates. Excel Power Query cleaned the records, Excel supported the analysis, and Power BI presented the operational metrics.

POSSIBLE RESPONSES

What could improve?

Evaluate afternoon fast-track options, coordinate inpatient bed availability and monitor LWBS alongside waiting time. These are suggestions for investigation—not proven interventions.
